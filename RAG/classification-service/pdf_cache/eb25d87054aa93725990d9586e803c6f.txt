## About Shri Vile Parle Kelavani Mandal (SVKM)
* **Registration:** Registered under (i) the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 – No. 733 of 1934-1935 and (ii) the Bombay Public Trusts Act, 1950 (Bombay) XXIX of 1950 – No. F-30 (Bom.) 1953.
* **Commencement:** Activities commenced in 1934.
* **First Activity:** Took over The Rashtriya Shhala (established in 1921), renamed as "Shrimati Gokalibai Punamchand Pitamber High School".
* **Institutions/Service Facilities:** 28 established to date.
* **Students:** Approximately 35,000 students across various institutions.
* **Faculty Coverage:** Arts, Commerce, Science, Law, Management, Technical Education & School Education.

---

## About Shri Bhagubhai Mafatlal Polytechnic and College of Engineering
* **Establishment:** Established in 1963 by SVKM, a Grant-in-aid Autonomous Polytechnic under Director of Technical Education and approved by AICTE.
* **Pattern:** Introduced sandwich pattern courses under partial autonomy granted by the State Government. Full academic autonomy from 1989.
* **Duration (Diploma):** 3 years (6 semesters) including 1 semester of In-plant Training.
* **Degree Programs:** Started Full Time 4 years B.Tech Engineering Degree Programmes from A.Y. 2024–25 (Started UG Engineering Degree Programmes with SI 240 in 2024).

---

## Vision & Mission

* **VISION:** SBM Polytechnic & COE aspires to be the lead Institute in providing need-based Technical Education.
* **MISSION:**
  * To provide state-of-the-art infrastructure and latest equipments for providing a stimulating learning environment.
  * To prepare students to meet the dynamic needs of the industry by periodic reviewing and upgradation of curriculum through an interactive process with industry.
  * To inculcate a spirit of excellence in terms of academic performance, research and innovation in faculty by providing appropriate support and incentive systems.
  * To promote and support co-curricular, extra-curricular activities and industry interaction to make students socially sensitive and employable.

## Eligibility Criteria

### A. First Year of Post SSC (Diploma Programs)
1. **Maharashtra State Candidature Candidates, J&K / Ladakh Migrant Candidates:**
   * Indian National.
   * Passed 10th Std. / SSC examination or equivalent with at least **35% aggregate marks**. *(Note: Candidates other than Maharashtra State are eligible for Institute Quota only).*
2. **NRI / OCI / PIO, Children of Indian workers in Gulf countries, Foreign Nationals:**
   * Passed 10th Std. / SSC or equivalent with at least **35% aggregate marks**.

### B. Direct Second Year of Post SSC (Diploma Programs)
* Maharashtra State Candidature: Indian National, passed 10+2 with Physics/Mathematics/Chemistry/Computer Science/Electronics/Information Technology/Biology/Informatics Practices/Biotechnology/Technical Vocational/Agriculture/Engineering Graphics/Business Studies/Entrepreneurship **OR** 10th + (2 years ITI) in ANY branch of Engineering and Technology.

### C. First Year UG Engineering Degree Course
1. **Maharashtra State Candidates:**
   * Indian National.
   * Passed HSC or equivalent with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects along with one of Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational/Computer Science/Information Technology/Informatics Practices/Agriculture/Engineering Graphics/Business Studies/Electronics/Entrepreneurship, obtaining at least **45% marks** (at least **40% marks** for Backward Class categories, EWS, and Persons with Disability belonging to Maharashtra State).
   * Must have appeared in **MHT-CET 2025** and obtained a non-zero score **OR** passed minimum 3 years Diploma in Engineering and Technology with at least 45% marks (40% for reserved/EWS/PWD) **OR** passed 3 years D.Voc stream.

## Rules and Regulations Summary

* **Cancellation & Refund:** Online applications for cancellation must be submitted. Refund issued after deducting ₹1,000/- towards processing charges if cancelled before the cut-off date. No refund (except Security and Caution deposits) if cancelled after 5.00 p.m. of the cut-off date.
* **Conduct and Discipline:** Full and correct statements required; suppression of information or false documents leads to disqualification. Ragging is strictly prohibited by law (Maharashtra Prohibition of Ragging Act 1999).

## Scholarships & Financial Assistance (Maharashtra State & Central Government)

1. **Rajarshi Chatrapati Shahu Maharaj Shikshan Shulkh Shishyavrutti Yojana (EBC):** 50% Tuition and 50% Exam fee for candidates with annual family income not more than ₹8.00 Lac.
2. **Minority Development Department Scholarship:** Financial assistance (up to ₹50,000/- per annum or actual tuition fee) for meritorious students belonging to minority communities (Muslim, Buddhist, Christian, Sikh, Parsi, Jain, Jews).
3. **Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vasatigruh Nirvah Bhatta Yojna:** Maintenance allowance for wards of registered laborers/marginal farmers (₹30,000/- or ₹10,000/- for 10 months depending on location/income).
4. **Government of India Post-Matric Scholarship / Freeship (SC, ST, VJNT, OBC, SBC):** 100% or 50% tuition fee benefits as per rules for income up to ₹8.00 Lac.
5. **Central Government Scholarships:** Prime Minister's Scholarship Scheme, AICTE Pragati (Girls), Saksham (Differently-abled), and Swanath Scholarships providing ₹50,000/- per annum subject to eligibility.
