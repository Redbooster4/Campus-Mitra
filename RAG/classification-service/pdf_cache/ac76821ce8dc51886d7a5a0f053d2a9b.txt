### Special Notes & Clarifications:

* **\*In lieu of the “Certificate of Indian Nationality” following documents will also be acceptable:**
  * The School Leaving Certificate indicating the Nationality of hazel candidate as ‘Indian’.
  * Indian Passport in the name of the candidate, issued by an appropriate authority.
  * Birth Certificate of the Candidate indicating the place of birth in India.

* **\*\* Domicile certificate:**
  * Domicile certificate issued by the Maharashtra State’s appropriate authorities will be considered valid.
  * The domicile certificate of the Mother of the candidate shall be supported with a marriage certificate and legal proof of change in name if any. Such candidates will be required to submit a birth certificate clearly mentioning the name of the mother.

* **Caste Validity Certificate:**
  * Candidates belonging to SC, VJ/DT (NT(A)), NT(B), NT(C), NT(D), OBC, and SBC categories should produce a **“Caste Validity Certificate”** issued by the Scrutiny Committee of the Social Welfare Department.
  * Candidates belonging to the **ST category** should submit a **“Tribe Validity Certificate”** issued by the Scrutiny Committee of the Tribal Department at the time of claiming fee reimbursement. 
  * If a candidate fails to produce the caste validity certificate by the concerned department, they shall be required to pay the fees applicable to the General Category candidate.

* **Non-creamy layer certificate:**
  * Backward class candidates belonging to **VJ / DT / NT B / NT-C / NT-D / OBC/ SBC** are required to produce a Non-creamy layer certificate issued by the competent authority valid up to **31st March 2020**.
